02 / FEATURED CAMPAIGN

Northstar Technologies — connected workplace.

A self-initiated social campaign exploring internal communication, collaboration, and a more connected workplace. The six designs share a unified typographic system, blue-and-white palette, and flexible layouts for education, promotion, and storytelling.

Portfolio concept: Northstar Technologies is a self-directed creative exercise, not commissioned client work. Any figures or events appearing in the artwork are illustrative and should not be interpreted as measured outcomes or real announcements.

Original concept artwork is shown above; images are optimized for web display.

03 / CREATIVE EXPLORATIONS

Beyond the campaign.

A selection of self-directed graphic design studies exploring photo compositing, imaginative visual storytelling, product imagery, and packaging. These are individual creative exercises, separate from the Northstar campaign.

04 / CREATIVE APPROACH

One brand system. Many messages.

01 / STRATEGY

Message first

Match the format to the content: a clear headline for awareness, a structured graphic for education, and a focused call to action for promotion.

02 / DESIGN

Recognizable identity

Keep typography, color, spacing, and imagery consistent while giving each post its own purpose.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY

Clarity on every screen

Use legible type, strong contrast, and intentional hierarchy to support quick scanning on mobile feeds.

05 / CONTENT FORMATS

Built for different moments in the feed.

Campaign announcements

Bold hero messages that introduce a campaign or establish a clear theme.

Educational content

List-based graphics and information design that break complex topics into digestible ideas.

Promotional assets

Resource, event, and call-to-action posts that connect the visual system to an audience need.

06 / PROCESS & TOOLS

From message to finished asset.

Plan the content

Clarify the audience, message, channel, and desired action before selecting a visual direction.

Design a system

Develop reusable templates, typographic hierarchy, and graphics that can flex across a campaign.

Prepare for publishing

Export platform-ready assets, review readability, and prepare accessible supporting copy.

Design tools and workflow: Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, and digital content production.

07 / OUTCOME

Creative consistency across a campaign.

The Northstar concept demonstrates a flexible visual system for awareness, education, event promotion, and resource sharing. The additional creative studies show a broader range of compositing, image-making, and visual communication techniques.
