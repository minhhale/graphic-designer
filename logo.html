Logo Design & Brand Identity.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Identity with a clear point of view.

These two logo projects demonstrate different approaches to brand identity: an expressive symbol for independent journalism in the Mekong region and a technical, industrial mark for cold storage solutions. Each design translates an organization's purpose into a recognizable visual language supported by typography, color, and practical brand applications.

02 / FEATURED IDENTITY

Mekong Independent — a voice that flows freely.

Mekong Independent is a nonprofit news organization focused on governance, the environment, and human rights across the Mekong region. The identity aims to communicate trust, independence, regional connection, and the vitality of the river.

01 / DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Build trust and recognition

Create a contemporary identity that works across print and digital journalism and resonates with regional and international audiences.

02 / CONCEPT

River, people, connection

Flowing waves cross the letter M; an abstract human form expresses independent voices, while layered blues evoke the shared Mekong region.

03 / APPLICATION

Design for flexibility

Develop a consistent system for mastheads, press materials, stationery, and social media avatars.

Process: Adobe Firefly supported exploration of fluid shapes and motion. The logo was refined in Adobe Illustrator, with Figma used for mockups and responsive applications.

03 / INDUSTRIAL BRAND IDENTITY

Genn Cold — precision in every degree.

Genn Cold provides cold storage solutions across data centers, food logistics, and pharmaceutical preservation. The design challenge was to create one recognizable mark that expresses engineering reliability, temperature monitoring, and cross-industry expertise.

01 / DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Signal technical control

Represent precision, stability, and performance across demanding cold-chain environments.

02 / CONCEPT

Turn the G into a gauge

A dial within the letter G evokes calibration and monitoring; brushed-metal styling suggests industrial durability and blue vapor implies cooling airflow.

03 / APPLICATION

Extend the visual system

Explore digital, print, stationery, and equipment applications using coordinated typography and color.

Process: Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop supported composition and refinement. Midjourney and Adobe Firefly assisted texture and lighting exploration, while Figma supported brand application mockups.

04 / CREATIVE APPROACH

From mission to mark.

Understand the brand

Identify the organization's mission, audiences, personality, and practical requirements.

Develop a visual concept

Translate key ideas into forms, symbols, color palettes, and typography.

Refine and systematize

Test recognition and usability across sizes, formats, and branded touchpoints.

05 / PROCESS & TOOLS

Human-led design, supported by modern tools.

Concept exploration

Sketch and explore directions, including AI-assisted reference and visual experimentation when useful.

Design refinement

Develop and refine letterforms, symbols, compositions, and color systems with Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop.

Brand presentation

Prepare brand guidelines and mockups that communicate intended usage across channels.

06 / OUTCOME

Two brands. Two distinctive visual languages.

Mekong Independent uses organic movement and human symbolism to express journalism and connection. Genn Cold takes a more technical approach, using gauge imagery and industrial finishes to express reliability. Together, the projects demonstrate how identity design adapts to different audiences and business needs.
