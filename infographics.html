01 / PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW

Designing clarity from complexity.

My approach to information design combines research, structured storytelling, visual hierarchy, illustration, and careful use of color. Whether explaining a technical process or communicating a sensitive news story, I organize information to help audiences understand the central message quickly.

This collection includes self-directed concepts, healthcare communications, and editorial graphics created for Radio Free Asia. The featured work below uses original project artwork.
