Northstar Technologies

Creating a Connected Corporate Communications Experience

Brand Identity • Employee Communications • Presentation Design • Intranet • Campaign • Digital

Northstar Technologies is a fictional corporate communications concept created to explore how a unified visual system can connect employees across multiple communication channels.

The project brings together executive presentations, employee communications, intranet experiences, engagement campaigns, and digital content within one cohesive visual language.

The Challenge

How can a large organization communicate complex information clearly while creating a consistent, engaging experience for employees across presentations, campaigns, intranet content, and everyday communications?

The Approach

I developed a flexible visual communications system built around clarity, connection, accessibility, and consistency. A shared foundation of typography, color, imagery, information hierarchy, and reusable components allows the identity to adapt across channels while maintaining a recognizable brand experience.

Deliverables

Brand System • Executive Presentation • Employee Intranet • Employee Engagement Campaign • Newsletter • Digital Signage • Email Communications • Information Design

My Role

Art Direction • Graphic Design • Visual Communications • Presentation Design • Information Design • Digital Design • UI/UX • Campaign Design
