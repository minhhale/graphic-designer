SELECTED WORK / EDITORIAL GRAPHICS

Graphics & Editorial Storytelling.

Turning complex global stories into clear, compelling visuals through thoughtful composition, symbolism, and information design.

Editorial GraphicsPhoto CompositingVisual StorytellingAdobe PhotoshopRadio Free Asia

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Make the story visible.

These editorial graphics were created to communicate complex political and human-rights stories at a glance. Combining photographic imagery, cultural symbols, and carefully controlled composition, each piece establishes context while keeping the central story clear.

CLIENT / ORGANIZATIONRadio Free Asia
DISCIPLINEEditorial graphics & visual storytelling
FORMATSDigital news graphics · Image composites
TOOLSAdobe Creative Cloud

02 / FEATURED EDITORIAL GRAPHIC

Visualizing the Wang Shujun espionage case.

A composite portrait that brings together the subject of the story and recognizable Chinese national imagery. The visual approach balances human detail with the geopolitical context of the reporting.

01 / CHALLENGE

Give context quickly

Introduce a complex espionage story in a single, recognizable visual.

02 / APPROACH

Use layered symbolism

Integrate flag colors and star motifs without obscuring the subject, bench, or cane.

03 / RESULT

Keep the focus human

Maintain an editorial composition that supports the accompanying reporting.

03 / HUMAN-RIGHTS STORYTELLING

Uyghur journalists’ families detained in China.

A photo collage highlighting relatives of Radio Free Asia Uyghur journalists who were detained or disappeared in China. Individual portraits form the outline of Xinjiang, while a dark, wall-like texture reinforces the story of separation and repression.

Design focus: Human-centered storytelling, photo montage, geographic context, and sensitive editorial presentation.

04 / GEOPOLITICAL GRAPHICS SERIES

China–Cambodia relations.

A pair of graphics exploring the political and economic relationship between China and Cambodia. National flags become the visual foundation, while a bowtie and blended-flag treatment offer two distinct ways to communicate the story.

05 / CREATIVE APPROACH

Clarity through composition.

01 / RESEARCH

Understand the context

Identify the people, places, and political relationships that matter to the story.

02 / CONCEPT

Find the visual metaphor

Use familiar symbols and photography to express the central editorial idea.

03 / DESIGN

Establish visual hierarchy

Balance imagery, contrast, texture, and negative space to guide attention.

06 / PROCESS & TOOLS

From reporting to a finished graphic.

Editorial brief

Review the story, determine the intended message, and select the most appropriate visual direction.

Image development

Combine photography, flag imagery, shapes, and textures in a unified composition.

Digital production

Refine contrast, alignment, and legibility for web-based editorial publication.

07 / OUTCOME

Editorial visuals that explain and engage.

Together, these projects demonstrate an approach to editorial graphic design that translates difficult subjects into visually accessible stories, while preserving the human context behind the reporting.
