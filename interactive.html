SELECTED WORK / INTERACTIVE DATA VISUALIZATION

Interactive Maps & Data Storytelling.

Turning complex election data into clear, exploratory experiences through interactive charts, geographic visualizations, and thoughtful information design.

Interactive ChartsElection DataData VisualizationEditorial Storytelling

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making political data easier to explore.

These interactive visualizations examine Myanmar's parliamentary election results and state-level electoral data. Instead of presenting dense tables, the work gives readers a way to compare political representation over time and investigate geographic patterns through direct interaction.

Designed for public-interest storytelling, the charts emphasize legibility, historical context, and the relationship between elected representatives and military-reserved seats.

Project
Myanmar Election Data Visualizations
Role

Format

Tools

Data clarity, interaction, comparative storytelling

02 / FEATURED WORK

Myanmar parliamentary election charts.

Paired semicircular charts visualize how the composition of Myanmar's Upper and Lower Houses changed across the 2010, 2012 by-election, 2015, and 2020 contests. Hover over individual seats and use the chart controls to explore the data.

The charts highlight the constitutionally reserved military bloc: 110 seats in the Lower House and 56 in the Upper House during the periods represented.

03 / CREATIVE APPROACH

Designed for comparison and context.

01 / COMPARISON

See change over time

Consistent chamber layouts and year selections make shifts in party representation easier to compare.

02 / INTERACTION

Details on demand

Hover interactions reveal party names and seat counts without overwhelming the initial view.

03 / EDITORIAL CLARITY

Show the structural story

Distinct visual treatment for military-reserved seats provides essential context alongside election results.

04 / INTERACTIVE MAP

Myanmar election data, state by state.

This geographic visualization presents election information across Myanmar's states and regions. Exploring individual areas reveals voter counts, candidate totals, and population figures, making regional differences easier to understand.

05 / PROCESS & TOOLS

From election data to exploratory visuals.

Data mapping

Organized chamber-level election results and regional figures into visual structures appropriate to each dataset.

Flourish customization

Adapted parliament-chart and map visualizations, refining palettes, seat representation, tooltips, and transitions.

Visual refinement

Used Adobe Illustrator for supporting visual assets and consistent typography across the election graphics suite.

06 / OUTCOME

Inviting exploration instead of passive reading.

The final set of visualizations provides an accessible entry point to complex political information. Readers can compare chamber compositions and investigate regional election patterns while keeping the broader historical and institutional context in view.

3 interactive views

Two parliamentary chamber charts and one state-level map, presented as a coordinated election-data storytelling experience.
