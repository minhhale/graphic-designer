SELECTED WORK / BRAND IDENTITY & EDITORIAL DESIGN

Lionchase - Investor Communications System.

A cohesive, accessible visual language for investor updates—bringing clarity, consistency, and institutional polish to complex financial and portfolio communications.

Brand IdentityEditorial DesignInvestor NewsletterDigital Communications

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building trust through visual clarity.

At Lionchase North America, I led the visual design of an investor communications system centered on the inaugural Lionchase IR Insights publication. The objective was to present strategic updates and portfolio information in a credible, modern, and easy-to-navigate format.

The resulting design system brought a consistent visual approach to the newsletter, interactive PDF, HTML email, and social teasers.

02 / FEATURED WORK

Investor communications, thoughtfully designed.

A selection of original Lionchase work, from the inaugural IR Insights cover to editorial storytelling, feature articles, and investor email communications.

03 / CREATIVE APPROACH

A scalable system, not just a single publication.

01 / HIERARCHY

Clear information architecture

Structured content into recognizable sections—including From the Desk, Feature Spotlight, IR Insight, and Portfolio Pulse—to help investors scan and navigate updates.

02 / CONSISTENCY

Reusable visual components

Established modular grids, repeatable layouts, and consistent typography for future investor communications across formats.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY

Designed for readability

Considered color contrast, logical reading order, and readable type treatments across PDF and email experiences.

04 / VISUAL IDENTITY

Built for trust and clarity.

The brand guidelines pair Lion Gold with Midnight Navy, Tech Blue, Graphite Gray, and Sage White. Editorial serif accents create a premium investor-relations tone, while accessible sans-serif text and monospaced data treatments keep information easy to scan.

Lion Gold · #C99A28

Type system: Playfair Display / Source Serif Pro for editorial headlines; Inter / Source Sans Pro for body copy; IBM Plex Mono for metrics.

05 / PRODUCTION

Designed across channels.

Interactive PDF

Editorial publication design with structured sections and clear information hierarchy.

HTML email

Brand-consistent investor updates designed for digital distribution.

Social teasers

Adaptable promotional assets extending the publication's visual language.

AI-assisted imagery was created with Adobe Firefly and Midjourney and refined in Photoshop. It supported conceptual technology visuals while protecting pre-release information.

06 / OUTCOME

More engaging investor communications.

The project delivered a reusable framework for quarterly investor updates and a more unified brand experience across investor-facing materials.
